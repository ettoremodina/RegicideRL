# Release scope

The publication release is centered on the completed rules engine, desktop game, control panel, and the Random, Heuristic, PIMC, and ISMCTS comparison. No additional model training is required to close the project.

PPO must be reported honestly as costly to train and weak in the experiments available to the project; it is not a release blocker and should not be enabled in the final comparison without a valid checkpoint.

AlphaZero remains future work. The interpretable lightweight agent distilled from ISMCTS decisions also remains future work. Both may be described in the future-work pages but must not be presented as completed or required for publication.

The public documentation and experimental report are English, navigable, and split into focused pages rather than one long document.
