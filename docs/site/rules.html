Jack tier · Learn the game

Fight the castle with the tavern deck.

Players cooperate against twelve face-card enemies. Every card is both an attack and a scarce defensive resource; winning means defeating four Jacks, four Queens, and four Kings before the team can no longer act.

Set up two decks

The castle

Separate the Jacks, Queens, and Kings. Shuffle within each rank, then stack the ranks so all Jacks are fought before Queens, and Queens before Kings. Enemy statistics rise by tier.

EnemyAttackHealth
Jack1020
Queen1530
King2040

The tavern

Aces through tens form the resource deck. Hand limits depend on player count: 8 in solo, then 7, 6, and 5 for two, three, and four players. Multiplayer adds Jesters; solo uses two Jester tokens outside the deck.

A turn in four steps

  1. Play or yield. Play one card, a legal combination, or yield when the cooperative yield rule permits it.
  2. Resolve suit powers. Effective suits heal, draw, shield, or double damage. The enemy is immune to its own suit until a Jester cancels that immunity.
  3. Deal damage. Add the attack value to the enemy. An exact kill recruits the enemy onto the top of the Tavern deck; an overkill discards it.
  4. Defend. If the enemy survives, discard cards whose total value meets its effective attack. If that is impossible, the game ends.

The four suit powers

♥HeartsRecycle up to the attack value from discard beneath the Tavern deck.
♦DiamondsDraw up to the attack value without exceeding hand limits.
♣ClubsDouble the damage dealt by the play.
♠SpadesPermanently reduce this enemy's attack by the attack value.

When a legal play contains more than one suit, each distinct non-immune suit applies. Hearts resolve before Diamonds; Clubs doubles damage but does not double the value of the other powers.

Legal combinations

  • Any single card.
  • An Ace with one non-Jester card.
  • Two Aces together.
  • Cards of the same value when their printed values total at most 10.

End conditions

Victory: defeat the final King. Defeat: a player cannot meet a required defense, or cannot legally play or yield. The implementation also enforces the multiplayer yield restriction and solo-Jester timing.

This page is a compact orientation to the mechanics implemented by the engine. The repository includes the full official rule sheet at rules/RegicideRulesA4.pdf.
